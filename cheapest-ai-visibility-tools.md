## How to compare prices fairly

1. **Price the engines you need, not the plan name.** Add every add-on.
2. **Count prompts per engine.** 15 prompts on four engines is 60 checks a run; 10 prompts on seven engines is 70.
3. **Check the cadence.** A weekly tool gives you about 4 readings a month per prompt, a daily one about 30. AI answers change between runs, so more readings means a truer picture.
4. **Credits vs flat.** Credit plans can cost more than the headline when you use them hard.
5. **What comes after the score.** A cheaper tracker that only reports can cost more in hours than one that writes the fix.
