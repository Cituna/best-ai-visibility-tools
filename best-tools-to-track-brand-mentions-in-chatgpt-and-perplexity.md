## Mentions, citations and sources: three different things

- **A mention** is your brand named in the answer text: "Try Cituna or Peec AI."
- **A citation** is your URL in the answer's sources. Engines often cite a page without recommending its owner, so a citation alone is not a win.
- **A source** is a third-party page the engine read: a listicle, a Reddit thread, a GitHub README. If your brand is missing from the sources, it is usually missing from the answer.

A good tracker records all three. Cituna labels a citation without a mention as "used as a source, not recommended" so you can tell the difference.

## Why ChatGPT and Perplexity disagree

They read different parts of the web. In our own tracking across 34 buyer questions, Perplexity leaned heavily on large SEO publishers and GitHub READMEs, while ChatGPT more often read vendor pages and comparison articles. A brand can be named by one and missing from the other on the same day, which is why tracking only one engine gives a false picture.

## How to check manually (and why it stops working)

1. Open ChatGPT (with search on) and Perplexity in private windows.
2. Ask the questions your buyers ask, worded as they would: "best CRM for dentists", not your brand name.
3. Note whether you are named, in what position, and which links are cited.
4. Repeat tomorrow.

Step 4 is where it breaks: the same question gives different answers run to run, so you need dozens of readings per question to see a trend. That is the job a tracker does.
